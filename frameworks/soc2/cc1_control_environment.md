# CC1: Control Environment

The Control Environment is the set of standards, processes, and structures that provide the basis for carrying out internal control across the organization.

## Status: 🟡 BYOE (Bring Your Own Evidence)

Jula does not currently automate the collection of organizational governance documents. To satisfy this criteria, please provide the following evidence in the `evidence-manual/CC1/` directory:

1. **Code of Conduct**: Signed acknowledgment of the company code of ethics.
2. **Organizational Chart**: Current structure showing roles and responsibilities.
3. **Background Checks**: Evidence of completed background checks for new hires.
